Navigating MTEB: choosing an embedding model for industrial RAG

By Malek Haroud· 2026-11-23· 4 min read

MTEB (Massive Text Embedding Benchmark) is a leaderboard, not an answer. Picking the top row and shipping it is how a RAG pipeline gets slow, expensive and wrong.

Which MTEB column actually matters?

MTEB aggregates many task families: classification, clustering, reranking, and — critically — retrieval. For retrieval-augmented generation, the columns that matter are the retrieval and reranking scores, not the headline average. A model can win on classification and still retrieve poorly on your corpus.

Three questions before the benchmark

  • Language and domain. Industrial maintenance documents are multilingual (FR / EN / AR / DE) and full of part numbers and abbreviations. Evaluate on your corpus, not a generic one.
  • Dimensionality. A 1024-dimension model costs more to store and search than a 384-dimension one. If the gain is marginal, the smaller model wins at scale.
  • Latency and cost. Embedding is per-document at ingest and per-query at runtime. The query side is on the critical path of every user request.

Measure what you ship

The right workflow is: shortlist 3–5 candidates, embed a representative slice of your real corpus, and measure retrieval quality on hand-labelled queries. That is exactly the kind of sizing decision the RAG-Architect tool is designed to structure for industrial maintenance projects.

Embeddings are not the whole answer

Even the best embedding will hallucinate if you let the generator answer without a retrieved source. Industrial RAG needs guardrails: anti-hallucination checks and source traceability — every answer cites the document it came from. In a GMP-regulated environment, a confident wrong answer is worse than a refused one.

The takeaway: choose the embedding for your retrieval task, and let the guardrails do the safety work.
