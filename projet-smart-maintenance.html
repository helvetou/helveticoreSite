Smart Maintenance

Industrial AI that fuses NLP, RAG and time-series to reduce unplanned downtime and optimise MRO inventory — with measurable ROI in 90 days in GMP-regulated environments.

What it does

  • Reduce unplanned downtime — anticipate failures before they stop the line.
  • Optimise MRO inventory — align spares and maintenance windows with real demand.
  • Fuse three signals — natural-language work orders (NLP), technical knowledge retrieval (RAG) and sensor time-series.

Where it applies

  • GMP-regulated environments — pharmaceutical and regulated manufacturing.
  • Asset-heavy industries — production lines, utilities and industrial equipment fleets.
  • Maintenance teams — turning unstructured manuals and work-order history into a queryable, cited knowledge base.

RAG-Architect — sizing tool (Projet 4)

Smart Maintenance includes a companion tool that dimensions a RAG pipeline for industrial maintenance — so the retrieval architecture is sized before it is built, not after.
